# Installing Fold on your phone

Fold can live on your Home Screen with its own icon, opening without
Safari's address bar or tab strip.

## iPhone and iPad

1. Open Fold in **Safari** (this doesn't work from Chrome or Firefox on
   iOS — only Safari can install to the Home Screen).
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

Fold now appears as an app. It runs full screen and shows up in the app
switcher on its own.

### You'll need to sign in again

The installed app has **its own separate storage**, so signing in inside
Safari doesn't sign you in inside the installed app. Sign in once more the
first time you open it from the Home Screen — after that it stays signed in
as long as you use it at least once a week.

### What it can and can't do

It works offline exactly as the browser version does: changes you make with
no signal are queued and sync when you're back
([offline](./offline.md)).

It **can't** send you notifications or sync in the background — iOS doesn't
allow either for an installed web app. Fold syncs while it's open, so
you'll see other devices' changes when you look at it, not before.

## Android and desktop

Not yet. Fold won't offer to install on Android or desktop, but it works
normally in any browser there — open it as a bookmark and everything
behaves the same.
