# The command palette

One field that reaches everything Fold can do. Press
<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>, start typing what you want, and press
<kbd>Enter</kbd>.

It's the fastest way around the app once you know your way about — no
hunting for a button, and no remembering which chord does what, because
the palette shows you the chords as you go.

## What's in it

Open it without typing anything and you see everything, in groups:

| Group | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Create** | New todo, New list |
| **Go to view** | Today, Tomorrow, Next 7 days, Summary, Search |
| **Go to list** | Every list you have, with its colour |
| **App** | Settings, Help |

Type to narrow it down. The groups stay, and empty ones drop away.

## It forgives typos

You don't have to spell a command exactly. "sumary" finds **Summary**,
"serch" finds **Search**, and "setings" finds **Settings** — which matters
when you're typing fast and watching the results rather than the keyboard.

It's forgiving, not psychic: it's matching what you typed against the name,
so dropping most of the letters ("tmrw" for Tomorrow) is a step too far.
Type the first few letters instead — "tom" is enough — and the best match
is highlighted straight away, so anything you reach for often is a few
letters and <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

## Moving around

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>↓</kbd> / <kbd>↑</kbd> | Move down and up the list |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>P</kbd> | The same, without leaving the home row |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Run the highlighted command |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Close without doing anything |

You can click a row too, and hovering one highlights it.

## On a phone or tablet

There's no <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> to press, so the palette has a
button: the **`>_` circle** floating at the bottom of the screen, next to
**New todo**. Both stay within reach of a thumb, and they're there whether
or not the sidebar is open.

On a computer with the sidebar collapsed, the same pair appears in the
same place. With the sidebar open, the `>_` button sits at the top of it,
beside **New todo**.

The keyboard hints along the bottom of the palette are hidden on a touch
device, since there's no keyboard to press them on.

## What it doesn't do

**It doesn't search your todos.** Typing "milk" won't find the todo about
milk — the palette is for commands, and [Search](./search.md)
(<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>5</kbd>) is for todos. Keeping them
apart means neither set of results buries the other.

**It doesn't act on a particular todo.** There's no "complete this" or
"delete this" in here. Those live on the todo itself — right-click a row,
or open it.

**It won't sign you out.** That's in Settings, deliberately: it's not
something you want a stray <kbd>Enter</kbd> to do.

## `Ctrl`, on every platform

Like the rest of Fold's shortcuts, the palette uses <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> — even
on a Mac. <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> won't open it. See
[keyboard shortcuts](./keyboard-shortcuts.md) for the full list, and for
when shortcuts stand down.
