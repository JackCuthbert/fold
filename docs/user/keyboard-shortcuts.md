# Keyboard shortcuts

Three, and no more than that. Fold isn't an app you live inside all day, so
the shortcuts cover the things you reach for often enough to be worth
remembering.

| Chord | Does |
|---|---|
| `Cmd+N` / `Ctrl+N` | New todo |
| `Cmd+Shift+N` / `Ctrl+Shift+N` | New list |
| `Cmd+/` / `Ctrl+/` | Open **About Fold**, which lists these |

`Cmd` on a Mac, `Ctrl` on Windows and Linux. The **About Fold** window (the
`?` at the bottom of the sidebar) always shows the right ones for your
machine.

## When they don't fire

**While a window is open.** If you're already adding a todo, editing one, or
in Settings, the shortcuts do nothing rather than opening a second window on
top of the first. Close what you're in, and they work again.

**New todo needs a list.** Today and Summary aren't lists — they're ways of
looking at todos that live elsewhere — so there's nothing for a new todo to
go into. Pick a list first.

**While you're typing.** Anything typed into a text box belongs to that box.

A closed or collapsed sidebar is no obstacle, though. That's exactly when
reaching for the keyboard is quicker than going to find the button.

## Why `Cmd+F` isn't one of them

It's the obvious next shortcut, and it's deliberately missing: Fold has no
search yet. Binding `Cmd+F` would take away your browser's own find and give
nothing back. When search arrives, so will the shortcut.
